A short poem about permission and delegation, written for the make.fun bounty **the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235**. It pictures the quiet cost of asking someone else to act for you: you send them out on an errand in the rain while you stay dry behind the glass, and everything gets done — except the walking, the wet sleeves, the small proud tiredness of having gone yourself.
